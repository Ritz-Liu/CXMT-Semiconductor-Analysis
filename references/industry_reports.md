# Industry References

## 1. Purpose

This file documents the industry and company materials used to provide background context for the CXMT IPO research note.

These sources support industry classification, business context, and qualitative interpretation. They are separate from the market-price data interfaces documented in `data_sources.md`.

## 2. Semiconductor Industry Background

### Semiconductor Industry Association (SIA)

Source:

- Semiconductor Industry Association
- *State of the Semiconductor Industry* reports

Purpose:

- Provides background on global semiconductor-industry conditions.
- Supports high-level discussion of semiconductor-market structure and industry trends.
- Provides context for interpreting the broader semiconductor ecosystem.

Use boundary:

SIA reports are used for industry context. They are not used as the direct source of the daily stock-price series in Figures 1–3.

## 5. Analytical Role of Industry Sources

Industry and company references are used to:

- define ecosystem roles;
- explain why specific securities are included in the sample;
- provide business and industry context;
- support cautious interpretation of observed market differences.

They are not used to:

- fabricate or replace missing price observations;
- calculate expected returns;
- calculate abnormal returns;
- prove causal transmission from the CXMT IPO;
- establish institutional buying;
- generate investment recommendations.

## 6. Interpretation Standard

The research note uses descriptive language.

Preferred wording includes:

- observed;
- suggests;
- may reflect;
- is consistent with;
- industry context;
- short-term market response.

Avoid unsupported wording such as:

- confirms institutional buying;
- proves causal transmission;
- guarantees cost pass-through;
- technical support;
- buy / sell recommendation.

## 7. Relationship to Market-Data Sources

Industry references and market-data sources serve different purposes.

- **Market-data sources** provide the daily closing-price observations used in Python calculations and figures.
- **Industry references** provide business, company, and semiconductor-market context.

The price-data source mapping is documented separately in:

`references/data_sources.md`
